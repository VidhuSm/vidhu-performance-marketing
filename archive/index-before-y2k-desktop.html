Clear answers about services, fit, budgets and the results shown on this page.

What performance marketing services do you offer?

Meta and Facebook Ads, Google Ads, lead generation, creative strategy and production leadership. I also provide white-label creative and design support to agency partners.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

My paid-media account management experience is with Indian businesses, especially local-service and lead-generation accounts. International agency partners can enquire about creative and design support.

What does campaign support include?

Depending on the project, it can include campaign setup and management, audience or keyword planning, prospecting and retargeting, budget pacing, optimization and practical reporting.

What happened in the school admissions case study?

Over seven months, the Meta click-to-WhatsApp campaign recorded 960 conversations at a reported ₹81 per conversation, with ₹78,000 in media spend. The client reported that the batch filled. Conversations are not counted as qualified admissions, and the result does not establish that ads alone caused it.

Is the ₹3,000 weekly amount your fee?

No. It is a recommended starting weekly media budget paid to the advertising platforms. It is separate from the management fee.
